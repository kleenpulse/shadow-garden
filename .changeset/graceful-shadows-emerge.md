Shadow Garden is fully open source under MIT, and the catalog reflects it: the free/pro tier concept is gone from the data model and every surface. All 76 components ship complete source, the sidebar filter is now All/New (old persisted filters migrate automatically), the free-components collection folded into the 16 that remain, and structured data declares `isAccessibleForFree`. Premium components live in a separate private repo.
