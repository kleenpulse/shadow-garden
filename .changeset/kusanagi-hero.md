A new hero template, taking the catalog to 163 (154 in production while Landing Pages stays hidden).

- **Kusanagi** (Hero Templates): a long sword planted in a foggy field of tall grass, under a wide serif title and a thin editorial frame with film grain. Wind rolls through thousands of blades of grass and the fog gets thicker toward the horizon. On load the fog lifts, the grass rises and the camera glides in to the sword (the `intro` control turns this off). A click or tap sends a cut out from the sword: the grass in its path is cut short, then grows back. The button draws the sword out of the ground and cuts the whole field in a ring. A drag turns the camera around the sword, and a `distance` control sets how close the camera stands.
